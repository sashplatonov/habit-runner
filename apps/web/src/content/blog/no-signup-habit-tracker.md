Creating an account takes time, requires trust, and generates email you'll have to manage later. For a habit tracker — something as personal and private as a daily routine — the registration requirement often feels disproportionate.

The good news: several habit tracking apps work immediately, without any sign-up. Here's what's available and what each trade-off looks like.

## Why No-Sign-Up Matters

The practical reason is speed. You're motivated to start tracking now, and a sign-up flow cools that motivation. Every extra step between intent and action is a chance to abandon the process.

The privacy reason is significant too. An account links your habit data to an identity. Without an account, your habit patterns are just local data on your device — not a profile on a server.

For many users, a no-sign-up habit tracker is the right default. If you later decide you need sync, you can create an account then. The data is already there.

## What You Should Expect Without an Account

**What works:**
- Full habit tracking and logging
- Streak counting and streak history
- Analytics and completion data
- Offline functionality (if the app is built for it)

**What typically requires an account:**
- Sync across multiple devices
- Cloud backup
- Web access from multiple browsers

For a single-device user, the account limitation is often irrelevant. If you track on one phone or one computer, you don't need multi-device sync.

## The "Optional Account" Model

The cleanest approach — and the one Habbit Runner uses — is to make accounts fully optional. The app works completely without one. The account adds sync capability but doesn't gate any core feature.

This is a meaningful distinction from apps that require registration to use even basic features. An optional account model respects that many users don't need sync and shouldn't be required to share personal information to get value from the app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I switch phones?

Without an account, your data is on your current device only. If you switch phones, the data doesn't automatically move. Options: export your data before switching (if your app supports export), or sign in with an account to enable sync before the switch.

### Is it less secure to use a habit tracker without an account?

No — different risks, not higher risks. Without an account, the main risk is device loss (your local data is gone). With an account, there's also the risk of a server breach affecting your data. Neither is clearly more or less secure; the threat models are different.

### Can I add an account later without losing my existing data?

Yes, in Habbit Runner. When you sign in for the first time, your existing local data is merged with any server data. You don't start fresh.
